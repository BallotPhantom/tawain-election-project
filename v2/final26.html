2026 縣市長最終預測 2026 final forecast

依《公職人員選舉罷免法》第53條，投票日前10日（台灣時間 11月18日 00:00）起至投票截止（11月28日 16:00）不得發布、報導、散布、評論或引述民調。本站的 2026 縣市長預測與民調在這段期間暫停顯示，開票結束後恢復，並與實際結果對照。
Under Taiwan's Election and Recall Act, Art. 53, polls may not be published, reported, distributed, commented on or cited from 10 days before election day (00:00, 18 Nov, Taiwan time) until voting closes (16:00, 28 Nov). The 2026 mayoral forecast and polls are hidden during that period and come back after the count, set against the results.
